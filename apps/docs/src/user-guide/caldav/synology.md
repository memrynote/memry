# Synology Calendar (CalDAV)

Synology Calendar runs on your Synology NAS.

## Connect

In [Settings → Calendar](/user-guide/settings#calendar) → **CalDAV**, pick **Synology** and type
your NAS's CalDAV address. Synology Calendar shows it in its CalDAV settings; it looks like
`https://nas.example.com:5001/caldav/`. Enter your DSM username and password, and press
**Test connection**.
