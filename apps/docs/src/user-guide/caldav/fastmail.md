# Fastmail (CalDAV)

## Create an App Password

In Fastmail, open **Settings** → **Privacy & Security** → **Manage app passwords and access**,
create a new app password, and give it access to calendars (CalDAV). Copy the password.

## Connect

In [Settings → Calendar](/user-guide/settings#calendar) → **CalDAV**, pick **Fastmail**, enter
your Fastmail address and the app password, and press **Test connection**.
