# iPhone: Free Use and Sync Plans

memrynote on iPhone works without an account. You can start writing right away, and add an
account and sync later from **Settings**.

## First Launch

After the two welcome screens, memrynote asks how you want to begin:

- **Start on this iPhone** (free): no account or email. memrynote creates a vault on this
  phone, and your notes, tasks, and journal stay on it.
- **Sign in and sync**: sign in with your email code or Google, then pick up the vault you
  already use on your computer.

You can switch from the first option to the second at any time. Nothing you wrote is lost.

## Turning On Sync Later

Open **Settings**. The account card at the top shows **On this iPhone · Free · Not signed in**.

1. Choose **Sign in** or **Create account**.
2. Finish the usual account steps: the email code, then your recovery phrase, or linking from a
   device you already use.
3. memrynote adds the vault from this iPhone to your account as a **separate vault**. It does
   not merge it into a vault you already have, so no note is overwritten.

Signing in is free, but sync needs a plan. Without one, the vault stays on this iPhone and
Settings offers **Unlock sync**. If your plan has no free vault slot, memrynote tells you and
keeps the vault local until you free a slot or upgrade.

## Plans

**Unlock sync** opens the plan list: **Plus**, **Pro**, and **Believer**, the same plans as on
desktop. See [How Sync Works](/user-guide/sync/how-sync-works) for what each plan includes.
You can pay in two ways:

| Where             | How                                                       | Price                                                     |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| **App Store**     | The **Subscribe** button. Apple bills your Apple Account. | The App Store price for your region, shown on the button. |
| **memrynote.com** | **Pay on memrynote.com** opens checkout in Safari.        | The memrynote.com price (Paddle), the same as on desktop. |

App Store prices are higher than memrynote.com prices because they include Apple's
commission. Both ways unlock the same plan on the same account, on every device.

You need to be signed in to buy a plan, because the plan belongs to your account. If you are not
signed in, memrynote asks you to sign in first and then returns to the plan list.

**Restore purchases** reconnects an App Store purchase to your account, for example on a new
iPhone.

## Managing Your Subscription

When sync is on, **Settings** shows your plan, its renewal date, your storage, and where you are
billed:

- **Billed through the App Store**: **Manage subscription** opens Apple's subscription settings.
  Change or cancel the plan there. To switch plans from memrynote.com or desktop, cancel the App
  Store subscription first.
- **Billed through memrynote.com**: **Manage subscription** opens the billing portal in Safari,
  the same portal as desktop and [the web account](/user-guide/sync/web-account).

If you somehow pay in both places, your account uses the larger plan. Cancel the one you don't
need so you aren't billed twice.
