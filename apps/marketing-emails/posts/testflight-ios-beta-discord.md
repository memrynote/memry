Hi everyone 👋

**Memrynote for iPhone is on TestFlight, and the beta is open to everyone.**

It is a native app on the same vault as your desktop, with the same end-to-end encryption. Your notes, tasks, journal and calendar sync to your phone, and the server still cannot read any of it.

**How to join:** enter your email at https://memrynote.com/ios-beta?utm_source=discord&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=testflight_tauri_2026_11&utm_content=ios_beta_cta and Apple sends a TestFlight invite to that address. You need iOS 26 or later and the TestFlight app.

**Free and paid:** the iPhone app is free and works on its own, offline, with its own vault. To see and edit your desktop vault on the phone, you need a paid plan. Sign in with the email you use on the desktop, then scan the code from Settings → Account → Link new device on your computer, or type in your recovery phrase.

It is a beta, so expect rough edges. Take a screenshot in TestFlight to send me feedback, or post it here. Android comes after the iPhone app, on the same core: <https://memrynote.com/roadmap?utm_source=discord&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=testflight_tauri_2026_11&utm_content=roadmap>
