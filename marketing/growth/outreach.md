# Outreach to list articles and awesome lists

Issue #3466. The pages that rank for our category queries are list articles and directories.
Getting memrynote into them brings discovery and the backlinks memrynote.com lacks. This file holds
the target list, one email draft per target, the awesome-list entries, and the reviewers to offer
free Pro. Nothing has been sent or opened.

Kaan sends every email and opens every awesome-list PR from his own accounts.

## How the list was built

On 2026-11-18, the 12 non-brand queries from `.pi/skills/growth-review/treg-checks.md` went through
treg (`crawl4ai.google.serp.organic`, US, top 20), for about $0.004 in total. From each result
page, this file keeps the editorial list articles and the directories. It leaves out:

- App homepages and app stores.
- Competitors' own "best X" posts (Supernotes, Notesnook, Reflection, Deep Journal, Alfred, Lindy,
  Nimbalyst, Atlas, eesel and similar). They list themselves first and will not add a rival.
- Reddit, YouTube, Quora and Facebook threads. A reply there from the maker reads as spam.
- Privacy Guides and AlternativeTo, which have their own issues (#3465, #3464).
- Articles about mobile-only apps or Android, since memrynote has no Android app.

Byline and last-update dates come from each article's page metadata on the same day. A contact
route marked "checked" was found on the site that day. "Not found" means the article page and the
site's contact page gave no route. Look for the author's public profile instead.
