## New Features
- 🔍 Interface zoom level — a new Zoom Level control under Settings → Appearance scales the whole app from 50% to 200%, with `Cmd/Ctrl` `+`, `-` and `0` shortcuts and per-device memory across restarts.
- 🔽 Collapsible bullet lists — hover a bullet that has nested lines and click the chevron beside it to fold or unfold them.

## Improvements
- 🧠 Mind map follows your outline — clicking a heading in the outline while the mind map is open now moves the map to that heading, and the map opens on the section you were reading instead of the whole picture.
- ➖ Smaller toggle arrow — the toggle block's arrow is now sized in line with the surrounding text and bullets.

## Fixes
- 📅 Accurate Google calendar list — calendars that aren't being fetched are no longer shown as ticked; they appear unticked and marked "Not syncing," and turning one on pulls its events in right away.
