## New Features

- 🖼️ Note cover images — add a cover photo or colour wash to the top of any note from Unsplash, a link, your vault, or your own file.
- 📋 Save a note as a template — turn any note you have already written into a reusable template from its `⋯` menu or the sidebar right-click menu, keeping its body, tags, and properties.
- ⚡ Insert templates while writing — type `/` and pick **Insert template…** to drop a template's body at your cursor without disturbing the rest of the note.
- 🎨 Set a note icon from the title row — pick an icon for a note directly from its page title row.
- 🎬 Video attachments — attach video files to your notes and play them inline.
- 🗂️ Archived tasks are visible again — see your archived tasks and bring them back out of the archive.

## Fixes

- 📅 Multi-day calendar items — events and tasks that span several days now render as a single bar across those days.
- 🗓️ Calendar opens on today — opening the calendar goes straight to today instead of replaying a stale session behind a dialog.
- 🏷️ Template icons carry over — notes created from a template now inherit the template's icon.
- 🗑️ Deletes stay deleted — deleted notes and tasks can no longer be resurrected by a sync pull, a delete on one device wins over a simultaneous edit on another, and deleting a checklist task removes its line from the note.
