## Fixes

- 🖼️ Cover picker stays open — Adding a cover from the Add details menu no longer closes the picker before you choose one.
- ↔️ Mixed-language notes read correctly — Each line in a bilingual right-to-left and left-to-right note now gets its own text direction.
- 📅 Google Calendar events come back — Turning a calendar off and on again, or reconnecting its account, now restores every event, and one failing calendar no longer stops the others from syncing.
- 🔄 Quieter Google sync — Holiday calendars are no longer re-synced on every pass, and calendars that cannot push are no longer re-watched.
- ✅ Tasks with an invalid schedule — They are skipped when pushing to Google Calendar instead of failing the sync.
- 🪟 Second launch opens the main window — Starting the app again brings the main window forward, and the sticky note shortcut works on Windows.
- 🔗 Links and notifications reach the main window — Deep links, notifications and dialogs open in the main window.
