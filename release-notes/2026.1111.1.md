## New Features

- 🧮 Advanced task filters — build saved filters with and, or and not rules, and they sync to your other devices.
- 📎 Inbox attachments sync — files attached to Inbox items now sync between your devices.
- 📆 On this day — the journal shows the same date from past years under today's entry, with an All years layout for reading each year in full.
- 🗂️ Notes and folders together — the manual sidebar sort orders folders and notes together, and a note with a same-named folder appears as one row.
- ➕ New note from a view — use the /new view slash item, or the New note button in a view block, to create a note in place.
- 🤖 Custom AI provider — inline AI can use any OpenAI-compatible endpoint.
- 🔍 Filter note references — filter backlinks and unlinked mentions by notes, journal entries, or tag.
- 📱 iPhone beta invite — desktop users can join the memrynote iPhone TestFlight beta from the app.

## Fixes

- 📅 All-day events on the right day — provider all-day events show on their own day in every timezone, and memrynote all-day events are written to Google and CalDAV on the correct day.
- 🏷️ Every note property in Settings — Tags & Properties lists every property with the notes that use it, and property types learned from your notes are no longer lost on the next launch.
- 🔁 Repeating task start dates — a repeating task's start date now moves forward with its due date.
- 📁 Folder notes in folder view — a folder's view now includes the note paired with the folder.
- 🔄 Saved filter order syncs — reordering saved filters now syncs to your other devices.
- 📥 Inbox refresh after sync — the Inbox refreshes after every sync, and link previews fill in on your other devices.
