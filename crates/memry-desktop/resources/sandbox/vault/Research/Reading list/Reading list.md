Every book note in this folder is tagged book: Author, Shelf, Rating and Finished are the tag's fields, and topics, owned and url are the note's own properties. The folder opens as a table, and the view switcher has a card gallery and a list of finished books. The same books also show up in the Book tag's table, grouped by shelf.
